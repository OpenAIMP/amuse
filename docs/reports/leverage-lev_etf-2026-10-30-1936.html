How a trade gets made

1. Screen
Find candidates across the watchlist or universe.
→
2. Regime
Trend structure + anchored VWAP pick the strategy family.
→
3. Timing
%B, RSI and squeeze confirm — or veto — the entry now.
→
4. Strikes
Fib levels and swing pivots place short strikes behind structure.
→
5. Size
1–2% max loss per trade; total heat under 40%.
→
6. Preflight
Buying power, events, fees — then your confirmation.

How the strategies generate income

StrategyStructureThe edge it harvests
Bull put spreadshort 0.10–0.20Δ put + long lower-strike put, 21–60 DTEkeeps the net credit (short bid − long ask) if the underlying stays above the short strike — harvests time decay and the premium difference between the two strikes
Iron condorbull put spread + bear call spread, same expirykeeps both credits if the underlying pins between the short strikes — harvests premium from range-bound price action on both sides
Asymmetric iron condorbull put spread + far-OTM call wing (≥8% above spot)bullish tilt; the call wing's premium helps finance the put spread
PMCC0.80–0.95Δ LEAPS (270–800 DTE) + 0.15–0.35Δ short calls (21–60 DTE)harvests repeated short-call premium against durable delta exposure, at a fraction of 100-share capital

Every structure is defined-risk — max loss is known before entry, no naked legs. “Return on risk” (credit ÷ max loss) is a screening ratio, not a forecast. Thresholds above are the live values from gates.yaml.

Column guide — what each value means for decisions
  • Price ($10–500 gate): names outside the band are rejected — too cheap = wide spreads, too dear = capital-heavy.
  • RSI(14) (0–100, gate 30–70): <30 washed out, >70 overheated — both rejected. 50–65 is the sweet spot (full 10/10 base points): momentum with room to run.
  • vs SMA50 (% above the 50-day): regime extension read. The base score peaks near +2.5% — riding the trend, not stretched. Deeply negative or extremely extended both score less.
  • Off 52w high (% below the 52-week high, gate ≤25%): near-highs = strength and clean overhead; far below = possible value trap.
  • ATR% (average true range / price, gate 1–6%): the volatility filter. Too low = premium too thin to bother selling; too high = moves too violent for defined-risk spreads. Doubles as the position-sizing input.
  • Vol ratio (20d avg volume / 60d avg): >1.0 = expanding interest, conviction behind the move; feeds 10 of the 30 base points.
  • %B (position inside Bollinger Bands): <0 below the lower band, 0–1 inside, >1.0 above the upper band = extended, rejected (don't chase). 0.5–0.9 earns +2 timing points: riding the band, not stretched.
  • Band width (% of middle band): no gate — context. Low = compression, high = expansion already underway.
  • Squeeze ●: bandwidth at its 125-day minimum — volatility is coiled, expansion likely. Design stance: wait (scores 0; premium is cheap here, the worst time for a seller to get paid).
  • vs Anchored VWAP (% from VWAP anchored at the 52-week low): must be ≥0. Negative = institutional money underwater — rejected for bullish income. 0–3% above earns +2 timing points (tight to institutional support).
  • Trend (pivot structure): UP = rising swing highs and lows — authorizes selling puts. RNG = no structure — condor territory, harvest range premium. DN = falling highs and lows — bullish premium selling rejected. Uptrend earns +3 timing points.
  • Cross: GC = golden cross (SMA50 crossed above SMA200 within 20 sessions): bullish regime confirmation, +2. DC = death cross: bearish, rejected. — = no cross in window.
  • MACD hist (12,26,9): MACD line minus signal. Positive (green) = bullish momentum, +1. Negative (red) = bearish momentum.
  • Confirm*: ADX measures trend strength, not direction; +DI/-DI provide directional context. SMI, Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, and MFI are related momentum measures; extremes can persist during strong trends. OBV and A/D direction are volume warnings, not reversal signals. These readings add context only and do not affect the score or qualification gates.
  • Gap (overnight, open vs prior close): ≥+2% gap up (green), ≤−2% gap down (red). Large gaps mean overnight news — check the catalyst before trusting the regime read.
  • Momentum (RSI state): oversold (RSI<30), overbought (RSI>70), else neutral. A state label, not a buy/sell signal — overbought names can stay overbought.
  • Value (% of 52-week range): ≤25% = beaten-down (technically undervalued), ≥75% = priced at the top (technically overvalued). This is chart position, NOT fundamental valuation — the data feed has no earnings, P/E, or balance-sheet data.
  • Bias (trend composite, votes/6): price>SMA50, price>SMA200, SMA50>SMA200, MACD hist>0, RSI>50, pivot trend UP. 4+ votes = bullish, ≤2 = bearish, else neutral.
  • Score (0–40): base 0–30 (regime: SMA position 10 + RSI sweet spot 10 + volume expansion 10) + signals 0–10 (timing: %B 2 + anchored VWAP 2 + trend 3 + golden cross 2 + MACD 1; squeeze 0). A pure ranking device — higher = more gates passed with better timing, not a probability or expected return.
